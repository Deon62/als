Something not working?

One inbox, read by the people who built the app. Below is where to write, what to put in the message so the first reply is the useful one, and the fixes for the things that go wrong most often.

01 / Where to write

Three addresses, so nothing sits in the wrong queue.

Pick the one that matches the request. They are role addresses on purpose, not a personal inbox, so a reply does not depend on one person being at their desk.

  • Support

    support@ardena.co.ke
    Anything broken, any payment that did not land, any question about a plan or a limit.

  • Privacy

    privacy@ardena.co.ke
    Account deletion, data access requests, and anything covered by the privacy policy.

  • Anything else

    hello@ardena.co.ke
    Partnerships, press, a university that wants to talk, or a feature you wish existed.

02 / What to include

Four lines that save a day of back and forth.

Most support threads are long because the first message was short. With these four, the first reply is usually the answer.

  1. 01

    The number on the account

    The mobile number you signed up with, or the Google address if you used that instead. Without it we cannot find you.

  2. 02

    What you were doing

    The screen you were on and what you tapped, in one sentence. “Uploading a 40MB PDF to my Anatomy unit” beats “the app is broken.”

  3. 03

    What happened instead

    The exact wording of any error message. A screenshot is perfect, and it never contains anything we cannot already see.

  4. 04

    The phone and the version

    Handset and Android version, plus the app version from Profile → Settings, at the bottom.

04 / Try this first

The five that come up most.

Each of these has a fix you can apply in under a minute. If none of them is your problem, write to us; that is what the inbox is for.

  • The sign-in code never arrives. Check the number for a typo, wait sixty seconds before asking for another, and make sure you have signal for SMS rather than only data. If it still does not come, sign in with Google instead and write to us.
  • You were signed out unexpectedly. One account works on one device at a time, so signing in on a second phone signs the first one out. If that was not you, change how you sign in and email support at once.
  • A payment went through but nothing unlocked. Reopen the app, which re-checks with Paystack. If it is still locked after a few minutes, send us the M-Pesa or Paystack reference and we will apply it by hand.
  • An upload fails or stalls. Check the file is under your plan’s size limit, listed on the pricing page, and that it is a PDF, image or slide deck. A scanned page needs to be legible to you before it can be legible to the tutor.
  • The tutor says it does not know. That is the app working. It answers only from the material filed under that unit, so if the topic is not in your library it will say so rather than invent one. File the lecture that covers it.
