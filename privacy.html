Privacy policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

ALS is built around a student's own coursework, which makes it a private thing by nature. This page says plainly what we hold, why we hold it, and how to make us stop.

Who we are

ALS (Ardena Learning System) is operated by Ardena, Nairobi, Kenya. For anything on this page, write to privacy@ardena.co.ke.

What we collect

What you give us

  • Your mobile number, to create the account and send the one-time sign-in code. If you sign in with Google instead, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Your coursework — the units, notes, lecture slides, PDFs, photographs of handwritten notes and past papers you file. This is the material the tutor reads, and it is the most sensitive thing we hold.
  • What you ask the tutor, and the answers it gives, so a conversation can continue where you left it.
  • Your timetable and calendar — classes, CATs, assignments, projects and exams, with their dates.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: name, email address, course, year, semester and a photo.

What the app records as you use it

  • Usage counts — questions asked, quizzes taken, pages uploaded, and your study streak. These enforce the limits on your plan and draw the usage screen.
  • A device identifier, so a plan bought by one student works on one phone at a time.
  • Payment records — the amount, date and reference of a subscription payment. We never see or store your card number, PIN or M-Pesa credentials.

What we do not collect

  • Your location. The app never asks for it.
  • Your contacts, call log or SMS messages.
  • Your photo library. Picking one image to attach gives us that image and nothing else.
  • Card numbers or M-Pesa PINs. Payment happens on Paystack's own page, not inside the app.

Why we hold it, and on what legal basis

What Why Basis
Mobile number, profile To create your account and sign you in Performance of a contract
Coursework and chats To answer your questions from your own material Performance of a contract
Usage counts, device id To apply plan limits and stop one plan covering many people Legitimate interest
Payment records To take payment and keep lawful accounts Contract, and legal obligation
Hashed trial marker To stop the free trial being claimed repeatedly Legitimate interest

Your coursework is not our training data

We do not use your notes, files, questions or answers to train any AI model, our own or anyone else's. They are not sold, rented or shared with advertisers. Nobody else on a Friends plan can see them — a shared plan shares the bill, not the library.

To answer a question, the relevant passages from your own material are sent to our AI provider to generate a reply. The provider processes them to produce that answer and does not retain them for training.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run ALS, and only what each one needs to do its job:

Service What it does What it sees
Paystack Takes payment by M-Pesa, Airtel Money or card Payment details you enter on their page, and the amount
Celcom Africa Delivers the one-time sign-in code by SMS Your mobile number and the code
Supabase Stores your uploaded files The files themselves, encrypted at rest
Google Sign-in with Google, and app distribution Your Google account, only if you choose that route
Our AI provider Generates answers from your material The passages relevant to the question you asked

Some of these process data outside Kenya. Where they do, transfers rely on the safeguards in section 49 of the Data Protection Act, 2019.

We will also disclose data where a court order or the law requires it — and we will tell you when we are permitted to.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open — everything stays, so the tutor keeps knowing your units.
  • When you delete your account — content is removed immediately. Encrypted backups roll over within 30 days.
  • Payment records — 7 years, as tax law requires.
  • The hashed trial marker — indefinitely. It is a one-way hash and cannot be turned back into your number.

Full detail, including how to delete without opening the app, is on the account deletion page.

Keeping it safe

  • Everything travels over HTTPS, and files are encrypted at rest.
  • Signing in on a new phone signs the old one out, so a stolen device loses access straight away.
  • You can require your fingerprint or face to open the app, from Settings.
  • Staff access to student content is restricted to what is needed to answer a support request you have raised.

No system is perfect. If a breach ever affects you, we will tell you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, restrict what we do with it, hand it to you in a portable form, or object to a use of it. Write to privacy@ardena.co.ke and we will respond within 30 days, free of charge.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya.

Children

ALS is built for students in higher education and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data; if we learn we have, we delete it. Users under 18 should have a parent or guardian's agreement before paying for anything.

Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will update the date at the top, and for anything significant we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
