About

A tutor that only knows what you taught it.

General AI will answer anything, confidently, from a syllabus it has never seen. ALS reads one library — yours — and every answer carries the page it came from. If your lecturer said it, ALS says it. If they didn't, ALS says so.

01

File it once

Notes, lecture slides, PDFs and past papers, filed under the unit they belong to. That library is the only thing the tutor reads.

02

Ask in your own words

Ask the way you'd ask a classmate. Answers come back with the lecture and page behind them, so you can check the source rather than trust the machine.

03

Revise against yourself

Quizzes are generated from your own material, not a question bank — so a wrong answer points at a page you can go and reread.

04

Keep the semester straight

Timetable, CATs, assignments and exams on one calendar, with a streak that counts the days you actually studied.

Questions

Before you install it.

Where do the answers come from?

Only from the material you upload. ALS does not answer from the internet or from a general model's memory of your subject — it searches your own library, and every answer names the lecture and page it used so you can go and check.

Is my coursework private?

Yes. Your notes are never used to train AI models, never sold, and never visible to anyone else — including the four people sharing a Friends plan with you. A shared plan shares the bill, not the library. The privacy policy sets out exactly who sees what.

What if it gets something wrong?

It can, and that is why every answer carries its source. Treat the citation as the point rather than a footnote: if the page it quotes doesn't say what the answer claims, you have caught it in one click. ALS is for revising, not for outsourcing the thinking.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. Plans do not auto-renew. When the 30 days are up, access reverts to the free limits until you choose to pay again — nothing leaves your M-Pesa without you starting it. See pricing.

Can I split it with friends?

That is what the Friends plan is. One person pays KES 1,250 and gets a code that seats four others, so everyone has the full top plan for KES 250 each. Seats can be freed and passed on at any time.

How do I delete everything?

Profile, then Settings, then Delete account — it takes effect immediately and removes your coursework with it. If you can no longer open the app, the account deletion page explains how to request it, and exactly what is removed and what is kept.

Bring your own coursework.

Install it, file one lecture, and ask it something only your lecturer would know. That is the whole test.
