Terms of service

Last updated 23 August 2026

These are the terms between you and Ardena for the use of ALS. Using the app means accepting them. They are written to be read, not to be survived.

1. What ALS is

ALS is a study companion. You upload your own coursework and it answers your questions from that material, citing where each answer came from. It is a tool for revising, not a substitute for attending, reading or thinking.

2. Your account

You need an account, created with your mobile number or a Google account. You must be at least 13 years old. Keep your sign-in code to yourself — anyone who has it can read your coursework.

One account, one device at a time. Signing in on a new phone signs the previous one out. Accounts are personal and are not to be shared or resold; the Friends plan exists for sharing, and it is the only way to do it.

3. Your material stays yours

You keep every right in the notes, files and coursework you upload. You grant us only the narrow permission needed to run the service: to store your files, process them so the tutor can answer from them, and show them back to you.

We do not use your material to train AI models, and we do not share it with anyone outside the services listed in the privacy policy.

You are responsible for having the right to upload what you upload. Do not upload material you are not permitted to copy, or anything unlawful.

4. What the tutor is, and is not

Answers are generated by an AI model reading your material. It can be wrong, it can miss context, and it can misread a badly scanned page. Every answer carries the source it used so that you can check it — please do.

ALS is not a substitute for your lecturer, and it gives no professional, medical, legal or financial advice. Academic honesty is yours to keep: using ALS to submit work that is not your own is between you and your institution, and their rules apply whatever ours say.

5. Plans and payment

Prices are in Kenyan shillings and each plan runs for 30 days:

Plan Price Covers
Free trial KES 0 for 14 days One person, once per person, ever
Focus KES 150 / 30 days One person
Synapse KES 350 / 30 days One person
Friends KES 1,250 / 30 days Five people, KES 250 each

The trial is once per person. Deleting an account and creating a new one on the same number does not start a second one.

Payment is taken by Paystack. Plans do not auto-renew — when the 30 days end, access reverts to the free limits until you choose to pay again. Nothing is charged without you starting it.

Each plan has limits — units, pages, questions a day, quizzes — shown in the app and on the pricing page. Reaching a limit pauses that feature until the period rolls over or you move up a plan. Your material is never locked away: you can always read, export and delete what you have uploaded, on any plan and after it lapses.

6. The Friends plan

One person pays and receives a code that seats four others. Whoever pays controls the seats and may free one at any time, which returns that person to the free limits. Every member's coursework stays private to them — the payer cannot see it. If the plan is not renewed, everyone on it returns to the free limits together.

7. Refunds

The 14-day trial exists so you can decide before paying, so we do not generally refund a period that has started. If the service was materially broken, or you were charged in error, write to support@ardena.co.ke within 14 days of the payment and we will put it right. This does not affect any rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012.

8. Fair use

Please do not:

  • Share one account among several people, outside a Friends plan.
  • Automate, script or resell access, or try to work around the plan limits.
  • Upload malware, or anything unlawful, or somebody else's personal data without their agreement.
  • Attempt to break, overload or reverse-engineer the service, or extract another user's data.

We may suspend or close an account that does these things. Where we can, we will warn first and explain why.

9. Availability

We aim to keep ALS running, but it is provided as it is. Features may change, and occasionally something will be down. We will give notice of planned maintenance where practical, and of any significant change to these terms or to what a plan includes.

10. Ending it

You can close your account at any time from Settings, or by following the account deletion page. Closing it deletes your coursework — permanently, and on purpose. We may close an account for a serious or repeated breach of section 8.

11. Liability

Nothing here limits liability for death, personal injury, fraud, or anything else that cannot lawfully be limited. Subject to that, our total liability for any claim is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before it arose. We are not liable for indirect losses, or for academic outcomes — a grade is not something an app can be answerable for.

12. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya, and the courts of Kenya have jurisdiction. If a clause turns out to be unenforceable, the rest stands.
