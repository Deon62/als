Terms of service

These are the terms between you and Ardena Platforms Africa Ltd, the company behind als. Using the app means accepting them. They are written to be read, not to be survived.

01 / What it is

A study companion, not a shortcut.

You upload your own coursework and als answers your questions from that material, citing where each answer came from. It is a tool for revising, not a substitute for attending, reading or thinking.

  • It reads one library, yours. Answers are built from the material filed under a unit, and nothing else is consulted.
  • It cites what it used. Every answer names the lecture and page behind it. That is there so you can check it, and checking it is the point.
  • It is not your lecturer. als gives no professional, medical, legal or financial advice, and a disagreement between als and your department is one your department wins.
  • Academic honesty stays yours. Using als to submit work that is not your own is between you and your institution, and their rules apply whatever ours say.

02 / Your account

One account, one person, one device at a time.

An account is personal. The Friends plan exists for sharing, and it is the only way to do it.

  • Created with your mobile number or a Google account. You must be at least 13 years old to hold one.
  • Keep your sign-in code to yourself. Anyone who has it can read your coursework.
  • Signing in on a new phone signs the previous one out. This is deliberate, and it is what makes a single-user plan mean something.
  • Accounts are not to be shared or resold. Doing either is a breach of section 07.

03 / Your material

Your material stays yours.

You keep every right in the notes, files and coursework you upload. What you grant us is the narrow permission needed to run the service, and nothing wider.

The processors we use are named in the privacy policy.

The licence you give us. Permission to store your files, process them so the tutor can answer from them, and show them back to you. It exists to run the service and ends when you delete the material.

What we will not do with it. We do not use your material to train AI models, ours or anyone else’s, and we do not share it with anyone outside the services listed in the privacy policy.

What you are responsible for. Having the right to upload what you upload. Do not upload material you are not permitted to copy, or anything unlawful.

04 / The tutor

It can be wrong, and it shows you where to check.

Answers are generated by an AI model reading your material. Nothing in this section is a disclaimer we hide behind; it is how the thing actually works.

  • It can misread. It can miss context, and it can misread a badly scanned page.
  • Every answer carries its source so that you can check it. Please do; that one tap is the difference between a citation and a decoration.
  • It says when it does not know. If your library has nothing on a topic, als tells you what it does have rather than inventing an answer.
  • A grade is not something an app can be answerable for. See section 09.

05 / Plans and payment

Nothing renews behind your back.

Prices are in Kenyan shillings and each plan runs for 30 days. Payment is taken by Paystack, which accepts M-Pesa, Airtel Money and cards.

Each plan has limits on units, pages, questions a day and quizzes, shown in the app and on the pricing page. Reaching a limit pauses that feature until the period rolls over or you move up a plan. Your material is never locked away: you can read, export and delete what you have uploaded on any plan, and after one lapses.

  • Plans do not auto-renew. When the 30 days end, access reverts to the free limits until you choose to pay again. No card sits on file and nothing is charged without you starting it.

06 / The Friends plan

A shared bill, not a shared library.

One person pays and receives a code that seats four others. Five people, KES 250 each, and five separate private libraries.

  • Whoever pays controls the seats and may free one at any time, which returns that person to the free limits.
  • Every member’s coursework stays private to them. The payer cannot see it, and neither can the other members.
  • It ends together. If the plan is not renewed, everyone on it returns to the free limits at the same time.

07 / Fair use

The short list of things not to do.

We may suspend or close an account that does these. Where we can, we will warn first and explain why.

  • Do not share one account among several people, outside a Friends plan.
  • Do not automate, script or resell access, or try to work around the plan limits.
  • Do not upload malware, anything unlawful, or somebody else’s personal data without their agreement.
  • Do not attempt to break, overload or reverse-engineer the service, or extract another user’s data.

    We may close an account for a serious or repeated breach of section 07.

  • Availability

    We aim to keep als running, but it is provided as it is. Features may change and occasionally something will be down. We give notice of planned maintenance where practical, and of any significant change to these terms or to what a plan includes.
