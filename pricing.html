The ones worth asking.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. Nothing auto-renews and no card sits on file. When the 30 days are up, access drops to the free limits until you choose to pay again.

What happens when a plan ends?

You keep everything. Reading, exporting and deleting your own material works at every tier. What pauses is the metered part: new questions, new quizzes, new uploads.

Can I move up mid-month?

Yes. The new plan starts a fresh 30 days from the day you pay, and the higher limits apply from that moment.

How does paying work?

Payment goes through Paystack, which takes M-Pesa, Airtel Money and cards. als never sees your card number or M-Pesa PIN; that happens on Paystack’s own page.

Do you refund?

The free start exists so you can decide before paying, so a period that has begun is not usually refunded. If the service was materially broken or you were charged in error, write to support within 14 days and we will put it right.
